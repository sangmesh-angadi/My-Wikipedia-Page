Sangmesh Angadi

Sangmesh Angadi (born 15 May 2005) is an Indian student, independent researcher, activist, entrepreneur, farmer, and technician. He has been active in social, research, and philosophical activities since 2022.

▶ Early life and education

Sangmesh Angadi was born in Kappalguddi, Raybag Taluk, Belgaum district, Karnataka, India, to M. G. Angadi.

He completed his schooling at Composite Sports Residential High School, Chandargi, and later pursued his Pre-University Course at SPOCO's Composite Sports Residential Science, Commerce & Arts PU College, Chandargi.

He is currently pursuing his bachelor’s degree at Tungal School of Basic and Applied Science, Jamkhandi, where he was active as an ABVP activist during his academic years.

During his 2nd PUC, he was academically strong in Biology and maintained a deep interest in scientific and agricultural studies.

▶ Religious views and philosophy

Angadi follows Sanatana Dharma and is a devoted follower of Lord Shiva. He has studied the Shiv Puran and often shares philosophical discussions and teachings with friends and peers.

He believes in discipline, originality, independent thinking, and spiritual growth.

▶ Research activities (2022–present)

Since 2022, Angadi has independently conducted research in multiple areas, though these works remain unpublished.

  • Political research and social analysis
  • Evolution of Sanatan Dharma
  • Lingayat studies and traditions
  • Agricultural experimentation and farmer-related studies
  • Biological and scientific studies

▶ Activism and protests

Angadi participated in several student and farmer-related protest movements. During his bachelor’s degree studies in Jamkhandi, he was associated with ABVP activities and awareness programs.

He also participated in KRRS-related farmer movements, including the historic sugarcane farmers' protest held in Gurlapur, Belgaum district. His interest in activism was influenced by his father, M. G. Angadi, who was also associated with farmer and social movements.

▶ Languages and interests

Angadi has expressed interest in learning regional Dravidian languages such as Tamil, Tulu, and Telugu, along with Marathi written in the Devanagari script.

▶ MG Groups

MG Groups is a future entrepreneurial vision conceptualized by Sangmesh Angadi. Angadi is a day dreamer who believes in turning visions into reality through consistent effort and independent growth.

The name "MG" is inspired by his father, M. G. Angadi, who is popularly known as "MG".

The concept behind MG Groups is to establish multiple companies under a unified identity as a tribute to his father’s legacy and influence.

▶ Views and philosophy

Angadi has stated that he is not personally interested in active politics and believes modern politics often functions similarly to a business network, though he acknowledges the presence of a few genuinely good individuals within the system.

He strongly believes in positive thinking and personal growth. According to him, when an individual works sincerely with positivity and consistency, circumstances naturally begin supporting progress and success.

He also believes that no person is entirely bad, but that situations and conditions influence behavior at particular moments.

Angadi emphasizes the importance of learning the basics of every field. He believes complete mastery takes time and consistency, and often says that “good things take time.”
